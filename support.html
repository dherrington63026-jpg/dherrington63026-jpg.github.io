Frequently asked questions

My progress disappeared. Can I get it back?

Your levels, stars and boosters are saved on your device. On the main menu, tap Save / restore progress to copy a code you can paste back in later, or on a new phone. If you play in a web browser, saved data can be erased when browser data is cleared, so installing the app or adding Huezel to your Home Screen is recommended.

How do I get 3 stars?

Finish a level using few pieces. The level card and the badge at the top right show how many pieces you need.

What is a Pure clear?

Fill a whole row or column with blocks of ONE color. It scores triple and blows up touching blocks of that color.

How do I earn boosters?

By playing: clearing levels (more on Hard), getting three stars, and solving the Weekly Challenge.

Does Huezel need an internet connection?

No. After the first install it works offline.
