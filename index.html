HUEZEL

How to play

Drag the three shapes onto the grid. Fill a full row or column to clear it.

PURE CLEAR: if the whole line is ONE color, it clears 3x and also blows up every touching block of that color!

JOURNEY: every stage has a goal (clear lines, make Pure clears). Each stage is a little harder. The WEEKLY CHALLENGE is a new, very hard puzzle every Monday. You get all week to solve it.

Clear on back-to-back turns for a combo. You fail if no shape fits.

No ads while you play. No lives. No timers.
