Huezel Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Huezel is a color block puzzle game. This policy explains what information the game uses.

What we collect

Huezel does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or any personal information on our servers.

The game saves your best scores, daily streak, sound setting, and the optional name you type for sharing only on your own device, in the browser or app storage. We cannot see it. Clearing the app's data or site data deletes it.

Sharing

If you tap Share Result, the game creates a text message with your score (and your optional name). It is sent only where you choose to send it.

Ads and purchases

The current version has no ads and no purchases. If a future version adds ads or a one-time purchase to remove them, this policy will be updated first to list the ad provider and what it collects.

Children

Huezel is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13.
